What to look for:
  An OEM widget (MSI Quick Settings, ASUS Armoury) declaring CompactModePriorityPlacement
  or IsDeviceWidget. Copy its exact value form into src/Package/Package.appxmanifest.
  If NOTHING declares CompactModePriorityPlacement, the far-left slot is won some other
  way and the manifest is the wrong place to keep looking.
